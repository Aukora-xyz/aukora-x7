GRANTS AND OUTCOMES
  CHECKED in Policy9Laws, Policy9Replay and Core9PolicyLaws:
    Both current signatures authorize a single exact-intent cancellation at the addressed head and
    sequence. Revocation accepts a digest only when it resolves to exactly one eligible grant.
    Successful revoke removes just that intent, changes no other grant or restriction, and preserves
    generation, without a collision-freedom premise. Expiry is an inclusive per-grant sequence test.
    It requires no sweep or mutation. At a fixed sequence other grants follow their own restrictions.
    A refused use makes no state change. An independently expiring grant still observes its own deadline.
    Revoked or expired intents cannot pass either delivery route or install. Once closed they
    remain ineligible across arbitrary commands until a newly signed exact-intent grant. Expiring
    grant and renewal messages cover the cutoff, both owner keys, generation, parent, seq and intent.
    Legacy grants omit the expiry. Rebind clears grants and restrictions and advances generation.
    Correction requires an earlier report for the same full intent. The history proof preserves each
    outcome across arbitrary command suffixes. The exported query yields raw 0/1/2 for original
    confirmed/failed/unknown, adds 4 for a correction, and returns 256 for missing.
    logs/r9/policy-wasm-2.txt checks the concrete core. Gate tests cover expiration and revocation of
    genuine install grants as well as an accepted install at the renewed cutoff. The round-eight risk
    and stamp suites also ran through Core9's ABI. Their own print labels date from round eight.
